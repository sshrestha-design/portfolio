Design Rules

  • 01. Form Follows Friction Remove friction by default. Add friction only when the user needs to stop and think before acting.
  • 02. Invisible Systems Hide the complexity. A deep system should feel simple to the person using it.
  • 03. Empathy is the Engine Understand the user's cognitive load. Design mechanics and interfaces around how people naturally process information.

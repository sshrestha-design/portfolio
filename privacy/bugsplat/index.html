Official Privacy Policy

Bug Splat

Package: com.designfoundry.bugsplat • Last updated: October 2026

Summary for Parents and Guardians:
Bug Splat is designed with young children, toddlers (ages 5 and under), and families in mind. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data. The app is completely free, does not show any advertisements, has no in-app purchases, and runs entirely offline.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Design Foundry handles user information in the mobile game Bug Splat. Your privacy and your child's online safety are our top priorities.

2. Children’s Privacy & COPPA Compliance

Because Bug Splat is aimed at children of all ages, including toddlers under 5 years old, we strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and Google Play's Families Policy:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect personal identifiers such as names, birth dates, phone numbers, email addresses, precise or approximate location, voice recordings, photos, or videos.
  • No Persistent Identifiers: We do not collect or transmit hardware IDs, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), MAC addresses, or serial numbers.
  • No Parental Consent Required for Data: Since zero personal data is gathered, stored, or processed, no data transmission occurs from the device.

3. Zero Data Collection & Offline Architecture

Bug Splat operates entirely offline on the user's device:

  • The game does not connect to any remote servers or databases.
  • All game states (such as high scores or settings) remain strictly local on your device.
  • No user activity or gameplay behavior is logged, analyzed, or sent to external endpoints.

4. No Advertisements & No In-App Purchases

Bug Splat is 100% free to play (F2P):

  • No Ads: We do not integrate banner ads, interstitial pop-ups, video ads, or rewarded ads.
  • No Ad Trackers: No advertising networks or marketing SDKs are packaged in the app.
  • No In-App Purchases (IAP): There is no store, microtransactions, or paid upgrades, preventing accidental charges by children.
  • No External Web Links: The game contains no outward links or social media integrations that could direct children outside the app.

5. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The application does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics engines (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), or crash reporting trackers that collect personal information or telemetry.

6. Parental Rights & Data Deletion

Because Bug Splat never collects, retains, or transmits personal data, there is no user data stored on any server to access, rectify, or delete. If parents or guardians have questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, they may contact us directly.

7. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect any regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with a revised update date.

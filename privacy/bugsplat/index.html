Bug Splat A Game For Toddler's Age 2-5 is built exclusively for young children, toddlers (ages 2 to 5), and families. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal user data. The app is completely free, contains no ads, has no in-app purchases, and runs 100% offline.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Bug Splat A Game For Toddler's Age 2-5 (Package: com.DesignFoundry.BugSmash / com.designfoundry.bugsplat), published by Design Foundry.

We are fully committed to protecting the privacy of our young players and maintaining transparency with parents and guardians.

2. Children's Privacy, Families Policy & COPPA Compliance

Because our application is specifically designed for toddlers and children aged 2–5, we strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and Google Play's Families Policy Requirements:

  • Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, geolocation, microphone recordings, camera footage, or user media.
  • No Device Identifiers or Telemetry: We do not request, read, or transmit the Android Advertising ID (AAID), IMEI, MAC address, Android ID, or any persistent hardware identifier.
  • No Account Creation: No sign-in, login, social media connection, or profile registration is required or supported.

3. Advertisements & Commercial Features

Bug Splat A Game For Toddler's Age 2-5 is completely ad-free and free of commercial solicitation:

  • No Ads: There are zero banner ads, interstitials, video ads, or rewarded ads.
  • No Ad SDKs: No advertising networks, marketing tools, or cross-app tracking libraries are bundled in the application.
  • No In-App Purchases (IAP): The game contains no digital store, microtransactions, or paid unlocks. There is no risk of accidental purchases by children.
  • No External Links: The app does not display web links, external browser redirects, or social media buttons accessible to young children during gameplay.

4. Offline Gameplay & Data Practices

The app operates entirely locally on your device:

  • No internet connection is required to play.
  • No data is transmitted over the network or saved to remote cloud servers.
  • All game states (such as high scores or gameplay progress) are stored locally on the device itself and can be reset at any time by clearing the application's storage.

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics engines (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), tracking frameworks, or diagnostic SDKs that harvest user information.

6. Parental Rights & Inquiries

Because we do not collect or store any personal data on external servers, there is no personal user data to review, modify, or delete. If parents or legal guardians have any questions regarding this policy or our child-safe practices, please contact us directly.
